## 1. Curupira

Brazilian comic book illustration style (in the general spirit of classic 1980s-90s Brazilian "gibi" children's comics, but with fully original character design, not copying any existing character), digitized traditional ink-and-watercolor art, NOT 3D render, NOT photorealistic. Bold black ink outlines with slightly imperfect hand-drawn line weight. Flat cel-shaded color blocks with subtle halftone/newsprint grain texture overlay, like an old printed comic page. Whimsical-spooky mood, not gory or realistic horror, exaggerated cartoon proportions, big expressive eyes, mischievous friendly-eerie expression, like a children's storybook villain, not a horror movie monster. Warm earthy Brazilian palette mixed with haunted-night accents: deep forest green, ochre/terracotta, indigo night blue, ember orange, moonlight cream. Vertical portrait composition, 1080x1350px, 4:5 aspect ratio, full bleed background, no text, no logos, no watermark. Character placed in lower two-thirds of frame; upper third kept visually simpler/darker to leave clean negative space for text added later. Curupira: a small forest spirit with fiery red/orange hair, reddish-brown skin, barefoot, mid-stride on a dark Atlantic Forest floor at dusk, twisted tree roots, fireflies, dappled fading light. His feet are clearly turned backwards, and he leaves a glowing trail of footprints behind him that visibly point in the wrong direction, curving off into the misty trees. Faint, barely-visible ghostly chart/dashboard glyphs flicker in the mist behind him. Mischievous, sly grin. --ar 4:5
