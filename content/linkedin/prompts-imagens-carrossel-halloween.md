# Prompts de imagem — carrossel "Monstros do folclore no CS" (Halloween/Dia do Saci)

Prompts prontos pra colar numa IA de imagem (Midjourney, Ideogram, etc). Formato: fundo de card, vertical 1080x1350 (proporção 4:5, padrão documento do LinkedIn), sem texto embutido na imagem — o texto (nome da criatura + o textinho) entra depois, por cima, no editor.

Estilo de referência: espírito de gibi brasileiro clássico (traço grosso, cor chapada, textura de papel impresso), tipo Turma da Mônica, mas com design de personagem autoral/diferente, pra não ficar cópia.

---

## Bloco de estilo (repetir em todo prompt, é o "DNA visual" do deck)

```
Brazilian comic book illustration style (in the general spirit of classic 1980s-90s Brazilian "gibi" children's comics, but with fully original character design, not copying any existing character), digitized traditional ink-and-watercolor art, NOT 3D render, NOT photorealistic. Bold black ink outlines with slightly imperfect hand-drawn line weight. Flat cel-shaded color blocks with subtle halftone/newsprint grain texture overlay, like an old printed comic page. Whimsical-spooky mood, not gory or realistic horror — exaggerated cartoon proportions, big expressive eyes, mischievous friendly-eerie expression, like a children's storybook villain, not a horror movie monster. Warm earthy Brazilian palette mixed with haunted-night accents: deep forest green, ochre/terracotta, indigo night blue, ember orange, moonlight cream. Vertical portrait composition, 1080x1350px, 4:5 aspect ratio, full bleed background, no text, no logos, no watermark. Character placed in lower two-thirds of frame; upper third kept visually simpler/darker (sky, canopy, water surface, or smoke) to leave clean negative space for text overlay added later.
```
